    Factors Influencing English Fluency Among Undergraduates

    Question
    Which demographic, educational, socioeconomic and exposure-related factors influence English fluency among Faculty of Science undergraduates at the University of Ruhuna?
    Approach
    Analysed a stratified sample of 383 undergraduates from a population of 1,850 across four academic levels and four degree programmes. Screened factors with chi-square and Mann–Whitney tests, then built a binary logistic regression model in R from 11 candidate predictors using purposeful selection with likelihood ratio tests. Evaluated with Hosmer–Lemeshow, McFadden's R², a confusion matrix and ROC/AUC, and wrote the report in LaTeX.
    Findings
    Five significant predictors: English-medium schooling, A/L English grade, A/L Z-score, media exposure and society membership.
    • 76.8%accuracy
    • 0.835AUC
    • 383respondents
    • R
    • LaTeX
    • University project

    Forecasting Monthly Retail Prices of Samba Rice

    Problem
    Forecast monthly retail prices of Samba rice in Colombo.
    Approach
    Built ARIMA and SARIMA models in R, with ADF stationarity testing, model selection by AIC and BIC, and residual diagnostics to check model validity.
    Outcome
    An accurate monthly price forecast, later presented as co-authored work at a departmental symposium.
    • 7.57%MAPE
    • 9.73RMSE
    • R
    • ARIMA / SARIMA
    • University project
    • 2023

    Optimising a Cleaning Staff Schedule

    Approach

    Outcome
