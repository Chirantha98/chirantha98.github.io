  1. Required team member (Req TM) calculator

    MAS Active – Linea Intimo, 2026

    • Excel
    • Office Scripts
    • Planning

    Works out how many quality-checking team members each sewing module needs on each date, using the daily quantities in the sewing plan.

    • Calculates the requirement from each product's SMV and rounds up, because people can't be split.
    • Office Scripts rebuild the whole summary automatically, so the sewing plan is never edited, however many modules and dates it grows to.

    Required team members = ⌈ (daily quantity × SMV) ÷ 600 ⌉

    600 = checking minutes available in a day
